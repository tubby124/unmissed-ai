# D429 — Demo → Signup Automation

## Summary
After a demo call (browser or phone), there's no automated follow-up. The call goes to `demo_calls` table, but nobody gets a SMS/email with a signup link, and there's no "try your own agent" trigger. This is a black hole between "wow" and "convert."

## Required
- After demo call ends, send an email via Resend with:
  - Thank you for trying End Voicemail
  - Link to start setup / try your own agent
  - Quick recap of what they heard
  - Pricing link
- Track sends in `demo_calls` or `sms_logs` with `direction='followup'`
- Optionally wire SMS follow-up for call-me demos (caller phone is known)
- Add a delay gate so we don't email during the call — trigger on call completion
- Make follow-up opt-out clear

## Acceptance
- After a demo call completes, the caller gets an email with signup link within 5 minutes
- Email content is warm, not salesy — "you heard it, now try your own"
- SMS option also wired when caller phone is known
- All sends logged for dedup
