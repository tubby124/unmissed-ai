# D433 — Landing Page Niches Focus

## Summary
The marketing site lists 7 niche industry pages (Auto Glass, Property Mgmt, HVAC, Plumbing, Dental, Legal, Real Estate) plus a generic. But the niche pages are not well differentiated — most just swap the industry name in a generic template. Only 2 niches have real examples with working lead cards (Auto Glass, Property Mgmt). The remaining pages are hollow. The site talks to everyone but resonates with nobody.

## Required
- Audit each niche page: does it have unique copy? A working lead card? A specific demo path?
- Kill or consolidate pages that are generic placeholders
- Focus on the 3-4 niches with the strongest proof: Auto Glass, Property Mgmt, Real Estate (Hasan's domain), and an "other" path
- Add specific pain points per niche: auto glass = insurance calls, PM = maintenance triage, real estate = buyer/seller qualification
- Add niche-specific landing page content with social proof
- Merge the "Industries" page into the homepage flow — don't make visitors click away to see if you serve them

## Acceptance
- Each remaining niche page has unique copy that speaks to that business owner
- Each niche page has a working lead card or demo
- The generic "other" path is honest about what it can do
- 3 or fewer top-level niche pages with clear differentiation
- The homepage niche selector directly shows lead cards without navigating away
