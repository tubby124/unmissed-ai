# Settings > Alerts tab can't read multichannel alert columns

## Origin (2026-05-29)

Two mount points render the same `AlertsTab.tsx` component, each with its own SELECT:

1. `/dashboard/notifications` — `NotificationsConfigSection.tsx:11` local SELECT. Includes `alert_phone, alert_email, alert_email_cc, sms_alerts_enabled, notification_filter_spam`.
2. `/dashboard/settings` Alerts tab — uses shared `CLIENT_CONFIG_SELECT` in [src/lib/clients/select-columns.ts](src/lib/clients/select-columns.ts). **Missing** `alert_phone`, `alert_email`, `alert_email_cc`, `sms_alerts_enabled`. Only `telegram_notifications_enabled` and `email_notifications_enabled` are pulled.

Effect: when an owner opens Settings > Alerts, the SMS destination input, email destination inputs, and the SMS toggle render blank/default regardless of saved state. They look like new fields the owner never set, even though they may have configured them on the dedicated notifications page.

Diverged during Phase E.6 Wave 1: the shared SELECT was extracted from settings/page.tsx, but the multichannel alert columns (added 2026-05-25 in commit `6ea7ab45`) were only added to the notifications-page-local SELECT.

## Fix — converge (recommended)

Add the four missing columns to `CLIENT_CONFIG_SELECT`:

```ts
// src/lib/clients/select-columns.ts — add before telegram_notifications_enabled
... alert_phone, alert_email, alert_email_cc, sms_alerts_enabled, telegram_notifications_enabled, email_notifications_enabled, notification_filter_spam, ...
```

One-line edit. Adds columns to a SELECT, no other reader cares about extra fields.

## Alternative — deprecate

Remove the AlertsTab mount from `SettingsView.tsx:543` entirely and direct everyone to `/dashboard/notifications` (already in the sidebar nav). Simpler IA, one place to look. Disruption: anyone who has the Settings > Alerts URL bookmarked.
