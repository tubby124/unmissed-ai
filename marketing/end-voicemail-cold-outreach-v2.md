## How to use

**Personalization tokens (mandatory before sending):**
- `{{firstName}}` — first name only, no Mr./Ms.
- `{{businessName}}` — legal/operating name as it appears on Google
- `{{city}}` — Calgary, Edmonton, Saskatoon, etc.
- `{{REAL_CLIENT_WIN}}` — swap with a real number: "Caught 14 missed jobs for an auto glass shop in Calgary last month — about $2,800 in work that would've gone to voicemail." Use Windshield Hub, Hasan Sharif (real estate), eXp Realty, or Urban Vibe (property mgmt) data.
- `{{competitorCity}}` — the next shop/agent/clinic your prospect loses calls to

**Send cadence per ICP:** Day 1 initial → Day 4 follow-up → Day 9 breakup. Then stop. Do not send a 4th. The breakup email out-performs the 4th nudge every time.

**Start with Solo Trades.** Reasons:
1. Owner = decision-maker (no committee, no IT review)
2. One missed call = $200-2,000 in real money they can feel
3. Buying cycle is days, not weeks — trades reply or they don't
4. Price point ($29/mo) matches what they already pay for an answering service that doesn't book jobs

Real estate is second priority (longer cycle, needs nurture). Clinics are third (front-desk politics, slower close).

**Volume rule:** 30-50 emails/day per ICP, max. Above that you start hitting Gmail/Outlook spam triggers. Warm up the sending domain for 2 weeks before doing 50/day.

**Phone targets (Hotmail/Outlook):** call first if you can. These inboxes filter cold email harder than Gmail.

## CASL compliance notes (read once)

Every email above includes the two CASL-required elements:
1. **Identification:** Sender name (Hasan) + business (End Voicemail) + physical address (1234 17 Ave SW, Calgary, AB T2T 0C5)
2. **Unsubscribe mechanism:** Reply STOP — must be honored within 10 business days

**Update the physical address** to your actual business address before first send. If you operate from home, use a registered Alberta business address or virtual office. CRA-listed business address is the safest.

Implied consent (CEM): existing business relationship within 2 years, OR conspicuous publication of email on a business website without a "no unsolicited email" notice. Most B2B prospects qualify under conspicuous publication, but document your source for each prospect (URL where you found their email + date).

Keep a STOP list. Honor it forever. One CASL complaint = up to $10M fine — not worth the risk on a $29/mo product.
