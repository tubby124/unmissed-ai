# D432 — Eat Your Own Dogfood: End Voicemail on endvoicemail.ai

## Summary
End Voicemail does not use its own product. The demo agent exists as a website widget (WebRTC) plus a "Call Me" path, but the company itself does not have a real phone number forwarded to its own AI. If someone calls the company's business number after hours or when no one answers, they get... whatever carrier voicemail says. Not the product.

## Required
- Buy a Twilio number for endvoicemail.ai as a client
- Set up a proper client row (slug=endvoicemail or similar)
- Configure the agent: business facts, system prompt, email alerts, forwarding number
- Add the number to the company's business line forwarding or website contact page
- Run a real forwarded-call test through the full path
- Document the experience as a case study / onboarding reference
- Screenshot the owner alert (email summary) for marketing use

## Acceptance
- Calling endvoicemail.ai's business number during off hours reaches the AI agent
- Agent answers in the company name, qualifies the caller, captures info
- Owner (Hasan) gets an email summary with the lead card
- The experience is documented as a walkthrough for future clients
